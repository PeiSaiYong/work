Minmed Digital Name Cards: flows and security

Phone mockups of what staff and recipients see in each flow, and visual explanations of each security measure. Everything comes from Technical Specification draft 0.2 (15/10/2026). Where the spec doesn't define something, the mockup says so instead of guessing.

How to read these mockups

Proposed
In the spec for review, not yet approved.
Pending
A decision is still needed.
Candidate
Named in the spec as an option, not chosen (for example Cloudflare Turnstile).
Example only
A number the spec gives as an example, not a setting.
[…]
Placeholder. The spec has not defined this wording or layout, so nothing was invented.
Not in spec
Something you might expect that the spec does not describe.

Alex Tan is fictional. Names, titles, numbers and times are made up.

Rule used in every phone

Text inside a phone is either wording quoted from the spec or a dashed placeholder. Explanations sit outside the phone, with the spec section they come from.

Icons, colours and layout are illustrative. The spec fixes the wording and behaviour, not the visual design.

Pages

1 · Card not verified

Four separate cases: sign-in or directory failure, new card waiting for approval, change not approved, live check failed. Plus how the three recipient results differ.

2 · Changed details pending

From Edit my details to approval: what staff see, what recipients keep seeing, editing again during review, and the exact-version rule.

3 · No longer with Minmed

Deactivation: what changes at once, what the recipient sees, and what can't be erased (old WhatsApp messages, saved contacts, images).

4 · Security measures

Every scenario in §7, with a request-path diagram, a CAPTCHA simulator, a token checker, look-alike addresses and the public-field filter.

Open questions found while building these

The mockups show each of these as a placeholder or a note. Answering them would let the placeholders be filled in.

  1. Wording listed in §8 as still to decide: sign-in failure, held application, CAPTCHA and blocked messages.
  2. Wording that isn't defined but isn't listed in §8 either: the departure notice under Card unavailable, the text under Temporary verification error, the retry or status-unavailable message, the warning when editing during review, the approval and rejection emails, the download-failure message, the deactivation email to manage@minmed.sg.
  3. Button labels the spec doesn't name: the sign-in button, the submit button on the change form, and any Try again button.
  4. Card unavailable page: is the person's name, photo or designation still shown? Is a check time shown?
  5. Temporary verification error page: §7.12 says no protected response is released, so these mockups show no details. Please confirm.
  6. New card waiting for approval: what does the staff card preview show before first approval?
  7. Where the staff view lives (which address) isn't specified.
  8. Can someone whose card is deactivated still sign in and see Card deactivated?
  9. The reviewer's and administrator's screens aren't described anywhere.
  10. Format of the Checked time (these mockups use "10:42 am, 15 Oct 2026" as sample data).
